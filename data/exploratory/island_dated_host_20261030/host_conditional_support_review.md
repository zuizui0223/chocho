# Host-recording conditional support review

**Scope: descriptive counts and conditional support only.** This joins already completed outputs and assesses what comparison could remain inside the fixed primary latitude-conditioned support. It fits no model, computes no effect or coefficient, enumerates no host null, draws no tables, computes no tail or confidence limit, and changes no selection, gate, margin, mask, score or outcome. Counts below are not evidence of a host-recording association.

## Result relevant to the next scientific step

There is recorded-host versus no-match variation among the variable cells, including within actual fixed species-by-longitude-by-latitude-zone rows. However, **every no-match comparator among the 89 variable allowed cells has unresolved catalogue concepts**. There are **zero complete-resolved-catalogue no-match cells in the variable support**. Consequently this support cannot presently provide a host-present versus host-absent comparison. Even a fully resolved catalogue no-match would still be a failure to find a matched indexed early plant record, not biological absence or evidence that a host was unavailable.

The next meaningful scientific work is to resolve the unresolved plant concepts and examine source/recording coverage for the particular no-match cells that remain variable. It is premature to turn the broad table of 336 recorded-host cells into a host-availability effect, or to declare a recorded-host versus unresolved-no-match comparison an ecological host-absence test.

## What this establishes, and what it leaves unestablished

The cell occupancy file establishes that each “variable” cell has a strictly positive but less than one exact occupancy probability in the already completed source-distance fiber. The group counts show necessary recorded-host label variation within some actual fixed rows and target columns. Some recorded-host observations occur in variable cells.

They do **not** prove that the total recorded-host count among positive cells can vary in a complete table while satisfying all row and island margins jointly. Mixed labels within rows and columns are necessary support information, not a guaranteed feasible complete-table statistic contrast: constraints can couple exchanges and make a candidate statistic invariant. This review does not inspect cycles, enumerate tables, compute a statistic range, fit a host model, or calculate any host-association probability. A future host test would require a separately fixed and justified estimand and a support/invariance check after the unresolved-concept and source-coverage problem has been addressed. No host test is warranted by these descriptive counts alone.

The source-coverage work should focus on the **72 unresolved-no-match cells in the 89-cell variable support**, particularly the 12 mixed fixed rows and seven mixed islands listed above, while retaining the full frozen catalogue and preserving all unresolved states. This prioritizes an existing scientific uncertainty; it is not a new outcome, roster, threshold, grouping, or gate. Resolving the source concepts may still leave nondetection and uneven botanical recording. A recorded compatible plant does not establish local larval use, and the source catalogue does not establish introduced status.
