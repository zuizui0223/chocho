## 3. Scientific consequence, what is supported and what is not

**Supports:** a substantial, spatially heterogeneous geography of potential *plant-provided* novel climate opportunity; demonstrable ability to predefine and run an observation-based within-species-continent test with exact source identities; a factual quantitative information shortfall for the current coarse GBIF country evidence.

**Does not support:** host limitation release at butterfly population level, realized butterfly climate-niche expansion, niche conservatism of the butterfly itself, temporal ecological shift toward novel environments, butterfly population growth or competition increase. Country-wide butterfly recording cannot place its individual in the mapped climate-novel host-resource subarea.

## Decision / next evidence that could discriminate

**Stop repeating country-only aggregate tests as evidence of realized niche evolution.** Next independent biological test should target **point-level geolocated, wild-verified butterfly detections and justified sampling-effort controls** inside versus outside *high-resolution* introduced-host range polygons, with dated host introduction and explicitly validated larval host-use/overwintering or repeated local recruitment as separate endpoints. If source access or stage metadata is inadequate, retain the study as a **potential-resource niche geography** paper, not an observed niche-shift manuscript.
