# Finer climate and geographic-tether counterfactuals for BCE-augmented butterfly resources

**Exploratory post-hoc macroecology protocol fixed on 19 October 2026 before inspection of the full stricter-null outcomes.** Study: 239 recorded butterflies, the original WCVP/HOSTS accepted-ID host network + 1,027 partially European BCE/Clarke host links across 81 butterflies, 365 WGSRPD3 climate-covered regions, CHELSA V2.1, and fixed TDWG Level3 polygon geometry. This is not a new butterfly field observation, an independently globally representative butterfly host data set, or a test of demographic population change.

## What the previous result does and does not establish

The source-verified full 2×199-chain null on the augmented recorded butterfly→host network preserves native plant ranges, each plant's introduced range degree by Family×continent×coarse BIO1/BIO12 2×2 strata, and regional introduced plant-family counts. For 235 eligible butterflies, observed climate-analogue opportunity was 9,949/14,961 = 66.500% under the native HOST-PLANT BIO1/BIO12 5–95% window (7,370/14,961 = 49.261% under 10–90%). The strict host-network null medians left **+0.972–0.977 percentage points** and **+1.571–1.599 percentage points** respectively ([qualified run 39221327128](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/actions/runs/39221327128)). In a fixed original-eligibility 234-butterfly comparison from the SAME MCMC draws, residuals were +0.982–0.993 and +1.566–1.592 pp.

Even this may be **a within-bin plant biogeography artifact**, not a nontrivial butterfly-host ecological mechanism. The current task strengthens the plant-side geographic/abiotic matching **without altering observed outcomes, focal species or plant records**.

## Three prespecified counterfactuals, identical baseline graph

1. **Finer temperature/precipitation matching (primary):** swap introduced plant×WGSRPD3 edges **only within plant Family×WGSRPD Level1×CHELSA BIO1/BIO12 3×3 continent-local quantile strata**. This preserves fixed accepted butterfly→host identities; each introduced plant's number of regional occurrences in each 3×3 climate bin; plant native exclusions; and every recipient region's introduced plant-family counts. No pre-filter of butterflies with positive residuals.
2. **Four-axis climate matching (sensitivity):** same edge constraints with four continent-local binary axes BIO1 (mean annual temperature), BIO7 (annual temperature range), BIO12 (annual precipitation), BIO15 (precipitation seasonality), giving 2×2×2×2 joint strata. This adds seasonal climate variables but is not a mathematically nested refinement of the BIO1/BIO12 3×3 climate bins; compare results as *alternative* nuisance controls, not nested likelihood tests.
3. **Spatially local climate and original-footprint tether (geographic sensitivity):** use the previous BIO1/BIO12 2×2 group strata; additionally allow each exchange only when the original and destination regions are within **1,500 km** great-circle distance based on the TDWG Level3 representative point and each plant remains within 1,500 km of at least one region in **that plant's ORIGINAL introduced footprint**. Reject swaps violating any native host exclusions, plant climate bin, recipient region-family degree or plant introduction degree. A symmetric local exchange with a frozen original-footprint tether is a geographically constrained, nonuniform reachable-state null; it does **not** sample all possible global rearrangements.

The number of *unique butterfly×region newly added opportunities* changes under each null: main response per draw is **O/A = climate-analogue newly supported butterfly×region cells divided by all newly supported cells for that draw**, not raw O. Climate-analogue rectangles are fixed at q=0.05 and q=0.10 quantiles of each butterfly's contemporary-accepted *native host resource* regional BIO1/BIO12 values. No fitted butterfly physiological niche. Evaluate both augmented-eligibility **235 species** and fixed-original-eligibility **234 species** (exclude only newly eligible *Satyrium pruni*), on identical chain states, without choosing a favourable panel.

## Reproduction and stopping gates

- Independently seeded chains 20261019 and 20261020, **199 draws per chain, burn = 10×37,869 swap proposals, spacing = 4×37,869 proposals**. The source edge counts and each stratum's plant degree and region×plant-family degree must be checked after burn, and early/middle/late during sampling.
- Both q thresholds × both species panels × all three null modes; report **each chain's** median and 2.5–97.5% null distribution (fraction), within-draw denominator range, the observed minus null-median in percentage points, approximate ESS, acceptance and moved-edge fraction. Archive full numerical draw series and only derived aggregate data (not WCVP/HOSTS raw source geography).
- In climate-stratified modes require at least **75% of source plant edges in viable swap groups**, min **10% original plant edges moved after burn**, each chain approximate positive-sequence ESS >=30 per response, chain medians within 2×max chain SD and ≥100 accepted swaps per sampling interval. The pre-analysis structural viability scan gave 88.1% for BIO1/BIO12 3×3 and 81.9% for four BIO variables 2×2×2×2; these values describe swappability, **not biological results**.
- Spatially local 1,500 km radius may reduce feasible moves substantially. Explicitly require ≥**5% of original edges moved** after burn, ≥**100 accepted swaps per spacing**, both-chain ESS>=30 for each outcome, chain median agreement, and exact source/degree and original-tether checks. Failing any gate returns **NOT EVALUABLE**, not a positive or negative ecological discovery. No post-hoc distance-radius tuning to achieve desired significance.
- Serial Monte Carlo add-one tails from two independent *chains* are not exact independent permutation p values. Do NOT infer butterfly-range expansion, adult production or larval fitness from any result; do not divide biological selection into additive components using non-nested nulls.

## Decision rule

If the prior +0.97–0.98 pp broad-climate residual shrinks to ~0 or changes sign under either well-mixed stricter null, classify the observed small butterfly-network residual as **sensitive to finer plant niche/geographic structure**, and promote **plant-side redistribution/filtering** as the main macroecological finding, NOT a butterfly-specific matching law.

If a small positive residual persists under independent eligible gates and both climate windows, label it **conditional potential-host-resource spatial organization**, not butterfly-specific evolutionary adaptation. Lack of more globally representative independent host catalogues, equal physical-area weighting, plant subregional occurrence effort and butterfly dispersal/occupancy remains a substantial generality constraint.
