## Current ecological contribution, without inflation
**What the project measures:** Global *potential larval host-resource geography* induced by recorded plant redistribution with fixed known consumer–host identities. Its main original comparative contribution is that the geographic consequences for consumers are not captured by diet breadth and are strongly conditioned on which host plants contribute and how plant distribution records define native resource baselines.

**What the project does not establish:** successful butterfly range expansion, new local plant consumption, adult recruitment, strong biotic homogenization of field butterfly assemblages, interspecific competition, or resilience from multiple hosts. Especially, a within-source regression whose coefficient is reproduced by a strict botanical plant-range null must not be marketed as a new ecological general law.
