## Island / port research line
- **Plants/hosts:** introduced plants and host-resource composition; source-validated island resource opportunities (+118.9% islands vs +40.9% mainland in an observational summary), port associations with general introduced plants and introduced larval-host candidates.
- **Butterflies:** 51 island landmasses, 1,275 unordered island pairs; later indexed butterfly assemblages have a positive host-similarity association (Spearman rho +0.300), but this is NOT an actual observed colonization route.
- **Physical transfer candidates:** 194 original botanical ports (182 qualified marine ports), observed ship AIS spatial density and wind, along with geometric/geographical baselines and electrical effective resistance where physically justified. AIS may proxy human-linked plant movement, winds may proxy insect transport; they are NOT interchangeable and neither alone proves transport.
- **Predictive validation:** double-endpoint-unseen island pairs, latitude/hemisphere and spatial-block controls, source-aligned circuit variables and structural placebos. [Draft #137](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/pull/137) retains a positive real-AIS held-out later butterfly prediction increment (**+1.0020%** alone and **+0.5446%** beyond wind in its comparison), but **100/199** geographically local graph-node placebo reallocations match or beat actual AIS alone; graph-label specificity and transport mechanisms are **not established**.
- Keep historical trade/port records, shipping corridors, wind fields, native-source circuit geometry and any Circuitscape/Omniscape marine method evaluations in chocho. No sewage variable is inserted into island-to-island sea resistance.
