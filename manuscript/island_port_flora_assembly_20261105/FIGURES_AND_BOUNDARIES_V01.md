## Source and publication ethics

The island-mainland resource reconstruction shares the pinned 239-species HOSTS×WCVP data model and worldwide total +54.9% with the independent GEB manuscript. **Do not claim the worldwide +54.9% result as a novel independent result in two papers.** Before using an island-focused article for submission, review manuscript distinctiveness, citation/overlap and data source permissions with all coauthors and, as required, the editors. The current branch deliberately does **not** edit, merge or submit the GEB paper.

No new speculative manuscript figures, butterfly arrival dates, nitrogen dose-response claims or port traffic figures should be added without original measured sources. This draft is ready for scientific review of structure, **not** ready for journal submission.
