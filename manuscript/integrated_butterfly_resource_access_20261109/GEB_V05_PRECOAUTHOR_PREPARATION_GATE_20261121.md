## Stage B — coauthor review (ONLY after Stage A is complete)

Provide the **same immutable source version** of 1) manuscript main PDF/DOCX (blinded as required for journal, an identifying review copy separately if necessary), 2) 4 main figures and SI S1–S8, 3) full evidence/limitation register, 4) reproducible reviewer data/code access and licences, 5) provisional title page, cover letter, contributor declarations and source rights register. Ask coauthors to confirm science, contribution, names/order/affiliations, disclosures, funding, and final sign-off. Approval is a human event; no software check can fabricate it.

## Stage C — submission (ONLY after Stage B)

Apply recorded coauthor revisions, rerun all numeric and document checks on the **exact final commit**, verify all author/signed statements and reviewer access, then let the approved corresponding author submit through Wiley Authors. This workflow does not submit, mark a draft ready for review, mint a DOI or create a public release automatically.
