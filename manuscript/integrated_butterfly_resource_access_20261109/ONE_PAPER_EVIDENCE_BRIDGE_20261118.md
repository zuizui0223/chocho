# One GEB butterfly paper: island–port–marine evidence bridge (18 November 2026)

**Working title:** *Island ports and marine geography accompany unequal butterfly resource gains from plant redistribution*. **Status:** one source-qualified, unsubmitted, coauthor-review Draft paper in PR #179; not an identified causal transport mechanism.

## The five Results transitions (different estimands, one ecological question)

1. **Native starting geography magnifies island opportunities.** Original fixed HOSTS×WCVP 239 butterflies: potential resource +54.9% globally (206 species), island +118.9% versus mainland +40.9%. Native host region coverage 16.0% island / 37.5% mainland, contemporary 35.1% / 52.8%; **mainland–island coverage gap 21.4→17.7 points**. Gap-filling fraction **22.7% island versus 24.5% mainland**: island advantage is baseline amplification, *not* more efficient plant introduction. Among 5,741 added island opportunity pairs, **2,271 (39.6%)** belong to the 59 butterflies with greatest *native* island-region host coverage, a resource-geographic-breadth rather than flight-distance classification.

2. **Ports track the introduced whole-flora supply pool.** WPI port richness associates with WCVP **whole alien flora** under distinct island/regression controls: **β+0.221** (126; native host-resource adjusted), **β+0.233** (123; native whole-flora adjusted), **β+0.2445** (98; botanical effort adjusted). Known host candidate β+0.1639 on the 98, but **no positive independent host-specific port surplus conditional on whole alien flora** (β−0.0415; p=.093). This is port-associated vegetation, not observed vessel-delivered seeds. Native-flora conditional *pairwise* port-proxy effects near zero do not establish a causal native-versus-alien contrast.

3. **Natural and human-linked distribution proxies must be cross-checked.** In 22 sufficiently trait-covered botanical islands, native minus introduced **EuDiS seawater-dispersal morphology +2.95 pp** (descriptive CI +2.04 to +4.53). For 104 botanical regions the archived uniform-ocean *circuit* predictor has an introduced-flora +4.239% versus native −0.110% held-out MSE gain. A different 104-island **shortest-water-path** model improves **both** native +2.234% and alien +1.775% on the same 5,355 pairs; two-endpoints-unseen intervals encompass zero. These do not prove *ocean currents moved the natives / vessels moved the aliens*. The coarse 2005 OSCAR trajectory pilot and extremely limited 2020 AIS route matches remain sensitivity evidence.

4. **Butterflies sometimes use the newly available food.** Independent Macaronesian fauna 41 living butterflies, **18 endemics, 0/18** included in the original 239. Eight source-selected endemic known-host species are not a representative replacement. Original photographed *Pieris cheiranthi* larvae on regionally introduced *Tropaeolum majus* (Tenerife wild; La Palma garden) show direct use in a named endemic, but no new host-present home-archipelago region, successful adult emergence attributable to alien hosts or population benefit. HOSTS island-locality alien-host use (42.4% versus 9.1% mainland) loses evidence for *extra preference* when plant-family supply is matched (−3.87 pp; interval crosses zero).

5. **Indexed butterfly geography has independent filters.** In a distinct **155-butterfly** recording-prediction panel, host geography improves later-index MSE **4.12%**, held spatial blocks **3.00%**; the independent 224-butterfly global assemblage-beta increment is very small (**+0.157%**). In the sampled 51-island butterfly network the uniform-circuit +5.082% and AIS/wind beyond-host +6.488% are positive **retrospective source fits**, but stricter 990-pair both-endpoints-unseen host/geography/effort conditions give only **+0.6776% for AIS alone**, and **−4.6986% for AIS+wind**. Different predictor baselines mean this is **not a direct numerical correction** from +5.08 to +0.68. The source-overlapping species-level port/resource ρ=.652 belongs in SI. None of these measures butterfly colonization.

## Manuscript and figure gates

**Main Results sequence:** island resource arithmetic → plant/port whole-flora supply → endemic actual use → independent known-butterfly forecast → paired native/alien marine geography and selected butterfly assemblage → historical first-index negative controls. **Main figures should depict these observational contrasts without causal transport arrows.** Legacy Figure 4's 51-island original +5.08% bars must be explicitly captioned as a source-selected model and accompanied by the restrictive double-unseen result, or moved to supplementary when updating figure art. The data-and-rights audit, final legends, word target (~5,000), three dataset/model references (EuDiS/legacy OSCAR/Circuitscape) and author approval remain submission gates.

**Consistency guardrail:** Preserve each original positive source estimate and subsequent adverse test, with their unit, baseline, and provenance; do not collapse 104 botanical regions, 51 butterfly landmasses, and 155 forecast butterflies into one cohort. The source field photographs are not a measured island-wide response.
