Preserve together in SI, never present as preference above available flora.

- **R28**, originally selected 17 port buffers: 31 butterflies recorded, 16 co-indexed compatible candidate plants, 8,525 butterfly observations/59 species×port pairs. These exact numbers are **currently absent from main and SI**, and remain non-causal source-registration co-occurrence.
- **R26** 51.4% report lacks pinned source cohort and **R30** historical 32-species 66/115 vs 60/109 disagreement. Both require source reconstruction; no publication-grade numeric claim.
- **R37** +26.363% potential butterfly-resource Jaccard predicted by whole alien flora and exact-margin 225-state null exceedance +7.389 points are *same-WCVP source coupled*; they belong in SI, not as independent observed butterfly biology.
- Original 8,909-plant Lepidoptera-network degree x alien-flora expansion positive (26.2% degree1 vs 65.9% degree6+, within-family/native-breadth rho +0.295) is distinct from 239-butterfly resource panel; without reporting-intensity/crop controls it remains a separately tested hypothesis rather than the paper's inferred transport mechanism.

## Source hierarchy for the GEB paper

1. **Potential partner geography:** fixed HOSTS repertoire × WCVP region, subject to the sample and source-availability selection.
2. **Botanical supply and geography:** matched island ports, WCVP whole native/alien floras and model-defined undirected ocean resistance, none of which identifies seed delivery.
3. **Independent but retrospective GBIF consumer records:** 155-cell indexed-record prediction versus separate 224-species community Jaccard; neither measures butterfly establishment.
4. **Named individual larval use:** Canary endemic Pieris cheiranthi consuming introduced Tropaeolum majus, not all-endemic prevalence or demographic benefit.

**Publication decision:** retain all 37 R rows and the PR181–187 source analysis in this inspectable repository audit. The GEB main article should keep only the model-independent positives and accurately connected context. The separate numbered GEB SI must carry source-matched negative and alternative-model checks and the previously missing strict 133/194 eligibility sensitivities.
