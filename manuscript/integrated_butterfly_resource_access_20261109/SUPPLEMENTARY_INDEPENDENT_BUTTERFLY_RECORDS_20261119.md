## S.G.1. Three separate resource and consumer responses

**Source-defined potential resource geography:** 239 butterfly species with documented HOSTS plants and WCVP v13 accepted regional distributions; 26,530 native-only to 41,083 contemporary potential butterfly×region opportunities, +14,553 (+54.9%), +118.9% on the original 126 insular botanical regions versus +40.9% on the original 237 mainland regions. These values share the HOSTS source network and must not be called actual butterfly occurrence, fitness or establishment.

**Independent later GBIF record indexing:** the original 155-species species×cell forecast, 208,482 evaluation cases, M3 climate/landcover/prior-record versus M5 plus known host resources and host-family composition. Species-equal MSE decrease 4.12% temporal, 3.00% spatial blocks and 2.74% at least 100 km from training within the spatial sample. Source-model plant availability was not independently dated before butterfly-recording period.

**Independent recorded community composition:** original GBIF DOI 10.15468/dl.umqmsf, **14,637,152 occurrences**, 224 indexed candidate butterflies, 169 source WGSRPD3 regions / 14,196 nonindependent pair comparisons. Original late (2018–2024) observed butterfly assemblage Jaccard predicted from original prior (2001–2010) butterfly overlap, geography, native resources, contemporary whole alien floras, historical exposure, climate and source butterfly recording effort. M2 included alien plants **not registered** as hosts of these butterflies (O); M3 added alien plants registered in HOSTS as larval hosts (H). O is not experimentally confirmed a true non-host class. Original H-given-O reduction in regional-heldout mean MSE **+0.0912908%**, versus the larger distinct host-gain percent in the 155-species species-cell index task. Source GloNAF/HOSTS community predictors were selected and observed retrospectively, so neither effect proves colonization or true changes in insect–plant interactions.

## S.G.2. Exact observed butterfly source and observation-intensity sensitivity

We recovered the immutable original 14.6M-GBIF-derived annual source from chocho original commit **dfd49cfcf9fb01a3db47cc611aae8cdd9c53cda0**. Source checks reproduced **91,882 positive species×region×year cells**, **5,390 regional annual effort cells**, every original 169-region MSE and late/prior recorded assemblage pair. The unadjusted mean butterfly-recorded Jaccard was **0.11421295** in 2001–2010 and **0.16788019** in 2018–2024, increasing by **+0.053667**. Under **499 randomized** source-conditional thinnings to within-region minimum period observation activity, that observed contrast fell to median **+0.015064** when datasetKey×calendar-days served as the effort proxy, or **+0.002914** when GBIF indexed-record count was the alternative intensity proxy. The fraction by which the *simulated raw recorded* contrast decreased (71.9% or 94.6%) is **not an empirically identified fraction of true biological homogenization explained by observers**. No repeated standardized visits, detection probability or true absence were measured; original periods span 10 versus 7 years.

Source: [annual effort conditional comparison](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/blob/main/results/gbif224_annual_effort_thinning_20261119/README_JA.md), [source-exact reproducibility run](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/actions/runs/44453480218).

## S.G.3. Refitting known-host H added to other alien O after early and late intensity thinning

All original fixed M0/M1/M2/M3 model values and 169 held-region scores were reproduced before new fits. The original M2/M3 GloNAF whole flora, H and O plant identities were held constant. We re-simulated both previous butterfly Jaccard predictor and later butterfly Jaccard outcome from the original species×region×period positive-record totals. Each of the two fixed effort proxies was evaluated with **29 source-derived simulation draws** with exactly the same original 169 regions, 14,196 pairs, original training-only standardized Ridge lambda 10 and fixed retrospective source late recording/effort covariates.

With datasetKey×day proxy matching, median H-after-O regional-heldout MSE decrease was **+0.214956%** (conditional Monte Carlo central range **+0.065888 to +0.470555%**), positive in 29/29 draws. Using indexed-count matching, median was **+0.437833%** (+0.154318 to +0.736163%), positive in 29/29 draws. The same source-positive direction therefore persists under these two assumptions, though the improvements are small and **do not represent 29 independent experimental or global replications**. Original background later observation-effort and record-count predictors remained unthinned as the source outcome was simulated; these retrospective model comparisons need not be fully consistent with a real detection process.

Source: [known-host recorded community refit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/blob/main/results/gbif224_thinned_HO_transfer_20261119/README_JA.md), [full 29×2 score artifact](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/actions/runs/44456355685/artifacts/14498146982).

## S.G.4. Geographic transfer to entirely unseen major regions

The same source model was also evaluated by excluding **each entire TDWG Level1** from training. This is a stricter geographical transfer comparison than a single region being unseen within its biogeographic group. The original eight-group world-equal MSE improvement from known-host H added to O was **+0.0971299%** unthinned. Following the two observation-intensity proxy simulations, median world-equal improvements became **+0.694514%** (conditional Monte Carlo interval **+0.147009 to +1.157047%**) and **+0.779306%** (+0.435518 to +1.618278%), with positive global average in 29/29 conditional draws under either proxy.

However, only **5–7 of eight** biogeographic groups benefited per source draw (median six). An explicit exception was **Level1=8 South America**, where the entire-source-region H-after-O MSE gain was **−2.98062%** on unthinned observations, median **−2.25165%** with datasetKey-day and **−2.07000%** with record-count thinning. The small source Level1=6 contains only two test source regions. These outcomes are neither all favorable nor eight independent geographic natural experiments: world folds have unequal source units and cross-world dyads appear in more than one test.

Source: [full biogeographic transfer audit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/blob/main/results/gbif224_thinned_world_transfer_20261119/README_JA.md), [exact source CI and original signed scores](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/actions/runs/44461719514).

## S.G.5. South American failure mode: cross-world calibration rather than within-SA host rejection

When all **20** original South American botanical regions were withheld, **3,170** test dyads included **190** within-SA and **2,980** SA–other-world pairs. The original *unthinned* H-after-O MSE improvement was **+1.059% within SA** and **−5.399% SA–other**. Following the same source-intensity simulations, the medians became **−0.496% within** and **−4.036% across** under datasetKey-day matching, and **−0.126% within** and **−2.697% across** under recorded-count matching. The cross-world results were adverse in 29/29 or 28/29 draws, respectively. These categories reconstruct the original world-group M2/M3 MSE exactly by their respective pair counts.

Original source GloNAF known-host H accounted for **24.06% median** of introduced accepted flora among the 20 SA regions, versus **23.56%** in the other 149 regions; lower *absolute* H richness (99 vs 167 median) accompanied lower *whole* alien richness (344.5 vs 647). Thus the source does not independently demonstrate a lower **relative HOSTS coverage** on the SA flora. Instead, plant and butterfly source pair distributions differ: H Jaccard median **0.1068** in held SA–other pairs versus **0.1738** non-SA training, while original prior butterfly Jaccard is exactly zero in **57.8%** of SA–other pairs versus **15.3%** training. On the source-unthinned SA–other test pairs, the independent observed butterfly Jaccard mean is **0.06447**, against M2 prediction **0.08281** and M3 prediction **0.08380**. Adding H increases a pre-existing source prediction overestimate. This **source calibration/extrapolation failure** is demonstrable; an ecological inability to use hosts, missing specific HOSTS data, historical routes or species-specific detection mechanism is *not* identified.

## S.G.6. Additional evidence tiers, omissions and noncausal interpretation

Source-constructed H versus O on **104 botanical islands** yielded registered-host alien-flora Jaccard source predictions stronger than the nonlisted alien-flora predictor for *HOSTS×WCVP-defined potential-resource composition*, including original E-model H-added-after-O +17.609% relative MSE gain. Under WCVP family × island-occupancy matched label swaps with explicit H/O richness controls (199 dependent shuffles), the source H-added-after-O E gain was +16.365% against a matched null median +7.699%; 9/199 null scores were at least as large (descriptive add-one rank 0.050). These are source-structure diagnostics, **not independent ecological butterfly observations**, and cannot be pooled with the GBIF H/O improvements. The stronger result from S baseline used target-derived contemporary richness and was retrospective.

Independently enumerated living Macaronesian endemics (18/18) were absent from the 239-source global network; source-documented *Pieris cheiranthi* caterpillars actually using introduced *Tropaeolum* in Tenerife (wild) and La Palma (garden) provide individual interaction evidence but not new island establishment or fitness benefits. Independently indexed butterfly histories do not imply first arrival: **145/317** originally apparent post-2010 first-index cases were already indexed before 2001.

No above result identifies a dated source ship carrying a known-host plant to a previously butterfly-host-free island, the successful larval establishment attributable to that plant, or increased butterfly abundance. The study compares **source potential opportunity, independent recording predictions and selected direct local feeding reports**. The original GEB v0.3 complete island–port–marine results and all supporting adverse checks remain in Supplement S.A–S.F and immutable source commits, rather than being erased.
