**Official policy:** [Global Ecology and Biogeography author instructions](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14668238/homepage/forauthors.html) mandate review-accessible data/code, a stable repository at publication (GitHub alone is not one), no author identifiers in reviewer material, and documented restrictions where genuinely necessary.

**24 November source-specific correction:** This is a rights-metadata *audit*, not a licence grant. GSHHG v2.3.7's distributor explicitly uses the **LGPL** family; do not substitute the third-party GPL-3.0-or-later label without resolving the actual archived licence text. The official NGA 2019 **27th edition** is verified for its publication; the source-used converted GeoJSON's exact edition and rights chain remain unresolved. The EuDiS parent record's CC BY 4.0 designation does **not**, by itself, replace checking the v5 source-used file and its rights. No raw third-party geometry or ports are newly released by this update.
