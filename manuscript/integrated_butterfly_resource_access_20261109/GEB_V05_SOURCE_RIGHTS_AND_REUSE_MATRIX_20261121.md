**Publication sequence locked:** Stage A all non-coauthor preparation including rights/data/metadata → Stage B coauthor scientific/author-input approval → Stage C exact-commit recheck and journal submission. Do not call the package 'ready' until the unresolved table rows have a source-backed resolution or a clearly documented editorially acceptable restriction.

**Official policy:** [Global Ecology and Biogeography author instructions](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14668238/homepage/forauthors.html) mandate review-accessible data/code, a stable repository at publication (GitHub alone is not one), no author identifiers in reviewer material, and documented restrictions where genuinely necessary.
