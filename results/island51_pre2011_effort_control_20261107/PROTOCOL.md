# 51-island pre-2011 botanical recording-effort control — fixed exploratory design

The previous island51 PR130 results, especially the positive host identity gain +2.2809% and circuit gain +4.0036% after candidate-host marginal richness adjustment, were known before this analysis. The previous 2001–2010 dated-host screening was also known. This is exploratory robustness, not independent preregistration or independent confirmation.

Population and existing predictors unchanged: 51 islands, 1,275 unordered island pairs, the original 225-butterfly candidate panel, 194 original botanical ports, 182 marine qualified ports; earlier butterfly Jaccard as a later-response covariate; original distance and port count controls; original candidate-host Jaccard pooled 2001–2024; uniform, native AIS and wind circuit fields; the two existing marginal candidate-host richness controls.

Only new input: archived 2001–2010 GBIF all-plant record counts from each of all 194 original port buffers, 141 positive queried facets and 53 source-confirmed zero year facets, all ports complete and assigned to 51 islands. Verify each port ID, island, source Git blob SHA and original count against archived audit before fitting. Sum counts across an island's original port buffers. Because buffers overlap, counts are not numbers of unique island plant records or plant taxa. The zero category is absence of indexed records, never ecological absence.

Two fixed response-independent pair predictors: sum and absolute difference of log1p(per-island summed pre-2011 port-buffer plant occurrence counts). Add both to old CR, HR, HCR, creating CRE, HRE, HCRE. Primary contrast CRE to HCRE: candidate-host composition beyond richness, marine circuits and early-era plant recording opportunity. Secondary HRE to HCRE: AIS/wind circuits beyond hosts, richness and early-era effort. Also retain CR to HCR, HR to HCR, CR to CRE, HR to HRE and HCR to HCRE. No other feature, endpoint grouping, threshold or transformation is selected after observing the outcomes.

Train-only standardization, OLS intercept, predictions clipped to [0,1], 51 leave-one-island-out folds (50 test pairs, 1,225 training pairs per island), two endpoint-specific predictions per original pair and model. Evaluate both later first-indexed butterflies (2011–2022) and earlier butterflies (2001–2010) separately; in the latter, early plant effort overlaps the butterfly response period and does NOT precede it. Report MSE, MAE, every adverse island fold, 612 model folds and 14 fixed comparisons. The original 306 C/H/HC island fold MSE and MAE values and previous six CR/HR/HCR global MSE values must reproduce before accepting new results; independently reconstruct 30,600 individual prediction errors and all comparisons.

Critical boundary: the original early species-by-port historical host incidence and accepted-name mapping are only archived in local files outside the public repo. This analysis does not replace pooled 2001–2024 candidate-host Jaccard with pre-2011 host Jaccard. Thus it does not eliminate post-target plant information leakage, nor fully resolve plant sampling, climate, actual larval feeding, butterfly colonization, shipping, or directional dispersal. Historical record counts are at best a survey-exposure proxy; port searches overlap. No independent-fold p-values or causal route claims.
