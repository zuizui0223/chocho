**Exploratory protocol fixed on 2026-11-19 AFTER the raw late-minus-prior community Jaccard contrast (+0.053668) and the +0.091% weak registered-host model transfer were known.** This is an identification diagnostic, **not** a preregistered test of genuine butterfly homogenisation, occupancy, colonisation or host-driven change.

## Declared effort-proxy thinning (before scores of this test are seen)
1. Within each fixed region r, sum the datasetKey×calendar-day counts in **prior** and **later** separately. Define a common proxy budget `E_r = min(E_prior, E_later)`. For period p use record-retention probability `q_rp=min(1,E_r/E_rp)`. This assumes positive species-level occurrence records within the same region-period are sampled at a rate proportional to the source **dataset×day** effort proxy. This assumption is strong and unvalidated: dataset-days are estimated using the focal butterflies, not standardized visits, and period lengths differ.
2. Secondary *sensitivity* uses **total indexed butterfly record counts** (rather than dataset-days) as the proxy with its own common budget `N_r=min(N_prior,N_later)` and q defined analogously. It may be even more response-dependent. It is not an independent ecological exposure.
3. For each source positive species×region×period record total `C_srp`, under independent Bernoulli thinning of records, probability that the species remains indexed is `P_srp=1−(1−q_rp)^{C_srp}`. Simulate **499** draws with frozen seed `20261119`. Every model uses **all original 169 regions** and all 14,196 region pairs per draw; do not remove islands or choose subsets by the outcome. Sampling each species presence from this probability is equivalent to thinning individual indexed records for that species given the model assumption (and avoids distributing third-party original rows). Report the resulting number of empty regional portfolios and dyads with undefined union **explicitly**.
4. Compute region-pair Jaccard similarities from the synthetic binary 224-species records in prior and later. Primary statistic: (later mean minus prior mean) across source pairs with a nonzero union, with strict denominators logged; sensitivity sets both-empty Jaccard to 0 with a reported flag. Also score pairs within the same TDWG level1 and cross-level1 separately. Report medians, 2.5–97.5 Monte Carlo quantiles, and the fraction of 499 draws where the difference is positive.
5. **Report raw and thinned** source species richness per period, the period-effort total and ratio distributions, fractions of regions whose later vs prior budget is reduced, and yearly effort heterogeneity. If source counts or source raw Jaccard do not match, fail *before* scoring. Do not select the thinning proxy, number of draws or denominator rule after observing results.

## Ecological limits and expected decision
The source annual table includes only positive human-observation records (not standardized repeated visits); datasetKey-day is **a recording-activity proxy**, not the number of surveys. Thinning by this proxy is a *recording intensity sensitivity*, **not a detection-corrected biological occupancy model**. Both periods cover different calendar spans and may differ in species-specific detection and observer behavior even after coarse equalisation. A positive result would not establish biological homogenisation and a negative result would not refute botanical opportunity expansion.

This test does **not yet re-fit the H-versus-O potential plant resource predictors** from contemporary GloNAF/HOSTS. It asks only whether the observed raw early/late butterfly **recorded community composition** difference is robust to two transparent observation-intensity assumptions. It leaves the original 155-species independent later-index prediction and 239-species potential-resource positive result untouched. A later model could link effort-thinned community response to known-host and other introduced-plant predictors, but only after this gate.
