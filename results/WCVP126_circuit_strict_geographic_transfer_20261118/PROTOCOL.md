# Strict geographically unseen test of the original WCVP uniform-ocean *electrical* circuit (source protocol)

**18 November 2026 | Source-locked follow-up on the already-inspected original 104-island results | Exploratory, not a preregistered external replication.**

## Frozen question and denominator

Does the source-authentic **uniform-ocean GSHHG finite-volume effective electrical resistance** retain a differential predictive gain for introduced versus native accepted WCVP plant assemblage Jaccard when **both botanical island endpoints** are absent from training? The previously inspected leave-one-island-out source estimates were **+4.23901365% introduced** versus **−0.10961037% native** (original CI #44013825017). The stricter geometric *shortest-water-path* double-unseen fit gave **+1.85765346% introduced, +1.73510723% native**; because that marine predictor is not electrical resistance, it does **not** answer this exact question.

**Same source input and folds:** source original 126 WCVP regions, exactly **104 marine-connected regions**, **5,355 pairs with native and alien Jaccard defined**, one eligible pair with an undefined Jaccard excluded for both outcomes. Reuse immutable original data workflows #44010307551 (accepted flora and contemporary WPI IDs), #44010673580 (GSHHG coastline source), #44013658955 (source-original uniform finite-volume circuit). For the exact five deterministic code-hash geographic bins, preserve the original double-unseen source protocol and model from #44011411479. At test time both endpoints are absent in **every training pair**; only same-bin pairs are test. Cross-bin pairs are unused that fold.

## Frozen model (no new response-selected tuning)

Use exactly the source's regression design and the **same ridge alpha=20** for both plant origins. Geography baseline B = log geodesic distance and square, broad geographic Level1 equality, absolute latitude difference, min/max pairwise WCVP accepted *native or introduced* richness (depending on outcome), min/max island area. The observed test-island richness is available, so this is conditional retrospective prediction, not historical plant arrival. P additionally includes the product of logged present WPI port counts. C uses `log1p` normalized undirected **uniform-ocean electrical resistance** in place of the former shortest-water-path detour. PC uses P+C. The only changed predictor between original geometric and new circuit models is the **marine descriptor**, not outcomes, sample, folds or penalty.

Scores = equal-fold mean held-out MSE and percentage reduction `100×(B−C)/B`, positive better. Also publish B→P, B→PC, P→PC, C→PC, fold counts, pair-weighted MSE as sensitivity, and **paired same-fold** descriptive bootstrap (5 island fold groups resampled identically for native and alien, 4,999 draws; Monte Carlo seed 20261118) of the *difference of within-response proportional gains* (alien minus native). This last statistic is descriptive: native and alien Jaccard differ in scale/source support, five bins are dependent and the source was inspected before choosing this comparison. No independent p-value from 5 bins.

## Immutable controls and decision

Before interpreting the circuit result, rerun the original geometric five-fold model and confirm the exact original source gains (+1.73510723 native, +1.85765346 introduced, 1,089 strictly held pairs) and identical B/P MSE and fold membership when swapping only the marine predictor. Do **not** optimize fold allocation, penalty, geography covariates, or outcome threshold to recover a positive result. Report negative and zero gains with equal prominence. If circuit gain disappears for introduced flora under this geographic test, retain original +4.239 as an exploratory *single-unseen* fit, not a transferable property of the ocean.

**No causal claims:** Original coastline-uniform circuits are **undirected water geometry**, not ocean-current vectors, historical ship voyages, source-to-destination plant propagules, or butterfly larval colonization. Even a positive prediction-gain contrast is insufficient to identify independent natural-vs-human transport mechanisms.
