2. **How different is the registered host / other alien flora predictor support?** Capture the original live GloNAF all-alien / known H / unlisted O region plant masks **during the unmodified original model run**, with independent exact-match checks against the source covariate matrices (whole alien Jaccard at original column 10, known-host Jaccard at original M3 column 22, unlisted Jaccard at original M2 column 22).
