## Two fixed observation-intensity assumptions
Same original 169 regions/14,196 pairs. Prior 2001–2010 and late 2018–2024, both fixed. Retain each species with probability 1 − (1−q_rp)^C_srp where C is the original summed positive indexed records for species s, region r, period p and q_rp = min(E_r,prior, E_r,late) / E_r,p under region-period effort E.
- **Primary proxy**: original datasetKey×calendar-day counts per region-period (days), as in the successful original annual effort sensitivity.
- **Secondary proxy**: original indexed butterfly record counts (records), which directly depend on observed outcomes.
- Fixed **29 paired Monte Carlo draws per proxy**, independent RNG streams seeded 20261119 (days) and 20261120 (records); **all 169 regions and 14,196 pairs retained**. Change **both the late observed community Jaccard outcome Y and the prior observed community Jaccard predictor X_prior in the same draw**. Other source-original predictors including actual late-effort and late-record counts remain frozen; the model is therefore *retrospective*, not a forecast, and its fixed nuisance measures might not be congruent with generated thinning.
- Missing butterfly portfolio unions (both endpoints empty) are not biological absence. Score them as 0 only alongside explicit counts and flags; report whether any draw contains undefined pairs. Do not discard an outcome-dependent subset and reuse 14,196 as a false denominator.
- Every outcome uses exactly the **original leave-region-out split**, 168 test dyads and 14,028 training dyads per region. For each draw restandardize features on training pairs only; original ridge lambda=10 and [0,1] clipping. Compare fixed M2 (original baseline plus other-alien Jaccard) versus M3 (M2 plus known-host alien Jaccard). No tuning or subgroup selection. Keep original M1/M0 benchmark, but optimize Monte Carlo work for this specific nested M2→M3 estimand.

## Output and evaluation
Each draw returns mean-region heldout MSE M2/M3, 100×(MSE_M2−MSE_M3)/MSE_M2, improving/worsening region counts, raw thinned prior/late Jaccard means, and empty region/pair diagnostics. Primary statistic: median H-after-O gain over 29 draws, range and 2.5–97.5 **conditional Monte Carlo** quantiles, fraction positive and contrast versus original +0.091290781%. The 29 simulations are **not** independent field studies, population bootstrap, or biological confidence interval. Site-level detection, observer identity, season, botanical arrival dates, host quality and true occupancy remain unobserved.
