## Fixed prediction design
The 51 original landmass IDs, all 1,275 original pair observations, 225 original butterfly candidate panel, original 194 WPI ports and frozen 15 longitude-block both-endpoints-unseen folds (235 within, 1040 between) are unchanged. Response one: later 2011-2022 butterfly Jaccard, earlier 2001-2010 butterfly Jaccard used as baseline predictor; response two: earlier 2001-2010 butterfly Jaccard for context only, where flora is contemporaneous, not temporally prior.

Original pooled 2001-2024 butterfly candidate-host Jaccard, candidate-host marginal richness controls, original distance/port counts and latitude/hemisphere controls, pre-2011 all-plant RECORD-COUNT effort proxies, original uniform circuit, and original native AIS circuit remain in original models.

Build general Plantae early-recorded island speciesKey set union across original ports. Pair early species Jaccard between nonempty sets. For pairs involving zero indexed early species use 0 as MODEL IMPUTATION only and add two explicit indicator controls for exactly one or both zero-indexed islands in all NEW models. Two marginal new general-flora speciesKey richness controls: sum and absolute difference of log1p indexed richness per island. All geography, recording effort, host/circuit controls unchanged.

Exactly six model designs, trained with original train-only z-standardized OLS intercept and predictions clipped [0,1]:
1. HRE_LAT = original PR134 host/effort/latitude baseline without AIS;
2. HRE_AIS_LAT = original baseline plus AIS; its comparison must reproduce all 6 original PR134 AIS-only target/scope MSE and MAE source values within 1e-9;
3. HB = HRE_LAT + early speciesKey marginal richness + zero-recording indicators;
4. HBA = HB + AIS;
5. HBF = HB + early general-flora Jaccard;
6. HBFA = HBF + AIS.

Six fixed contrasts: HRE_LAT to HRE_AIS_LAT (parent), HRE_LAT to HB (early richness/coverage), HB to HBF (early floristic identity), HBA to HBFA (early floristic identity conditional on AIS), HB to HBA (AIS beyond early richness/coverage), HBF to HBFA (AIS beyond early general-flora composition). Compare both butterfly periods and full 1275, within 235 and between 1040 longitude source scopes. Also report four-scope subset both islands having at least one early indexed plant species (no cherry-picking) separately for each model and contrast. Number output predictions: 2 targets × 6 models × 1275 source pair predictions = 15,300. Model-fold losses 180; 6 contrasts × 2 targets × 15 folds = 180 contrast-fold losses; primary results 6×2×3 = 36; optional both-observed subset adds 12, total 48 when subset nonempty. All pair observations must be tested once per model, and neither test island appears in training. Retain all positive and negative results and absolute MSE/MAE. Pooled original candidate host Jaccard 2001-2024 may postdate later butterflies, so adding early general-flora information does NOT repair all historical predictor leakage.
