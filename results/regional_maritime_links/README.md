# Regional extension of the annual direct-link test

The 27-destination panel, 132 species, 200 candidate source islands, static 2001–2010 regional introduced-host screen and 2019–2023 outcomes are unchanged. The matched unit is a species on a destination island in a particular year. A linked row requires service from an earlier same-species island. An unconfirmed row requires inbound coverage at the destination plus a prior conspecific island with outbound coverage in the same annual registry. One island can contribute to both groups. Survey and vessel coverage differ among registries.

## Sources and exclusions

- British Columbia operator December traffic statistics, routes 06 (Crofton–Vesuvius Bay) and 11 (Prince Rupert–Skidegate), for each calendar year. These support endpoints on Vancouver and Graham islands. The other terminals lie outside the fixed 200 candidate islands.
- Washington State Ferries quarterly ridership, first positive quarter for the Mukilteo–Clinton and Fauntleroy–Vashon segments in each year. The island endpoints are Whidbey and Vashon. Neither mainland counterpart is an eligible earlier island.
- Japanese Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Hokkaido passenger statistics, Hakodate–Aomori, FY ending March 2019–2023. Adds early coverage for Honshu–Hokkaido. Ferry totals establish service presence and are never converted to directional voyage counts.
- Balearic Port Authority berth records. The 61 monthly files (January 2019–January 2024) span complete calls with arrivals in 2019–2023; arrivals entered in the following month are included. Accept named port codes A/P (Mallorca), I (Ibiza), F (Formentera), M (Menorca); only Mallorca belongs to the fixed destination/source frame among those local islands. Require actual arrival and departure fields and a call ID, deduplicate by local port and call ID, and map the reported origin UN/LOCODE. All vessel classes are eligible. Multiple calls produce binary annual links. Unresolved origin codes cannot establish a link. UN/LOCODE points require a unique island intersected or identified within 5 km by a same-country WPI port.
- These regional records supplement the original sources; duplicate confirmations are collapsed. Hashes and original URLs are in [sources.csv](sources.csv). [positive_routes.csv](positive_routes.csv) lists positive traffic counts and units. Counts from different units and time windows are not summed as traffic volume.

## Reading the comparisons

The fixed geographic distance is from the destination to the closest island indexed for the butterfly by the previous year. The baseline matches within year at a distance ratio ≤1.25. The 250-km comparison limits each member's nearest-source distance. The Denmark sensitivity removes Danish islands as destinations and as eligible origins of transport evidence; it retains the original geographic-distance measure. Paired examples can cross regions or species. The probabilities shown describe recording within the same year. Annual data do not establish whether a voyage preceded an observation.

[matched_summary.csv](matched_summary.csv) gives one outcome-blind optimal solution and the extrema among distance-tied alternatives. Island counts describe the displayed solution. [matched_pairs.csv](matched_pairs.csv) preserves year, species keys, destinations, earlier linked island IDs, distances and sources. [crude_rates.csv](crude_rates.csv) gives the unmatched denominators. Run `python scripts/analyze_annual_maritime_links_27.py PATH_TO_BARRIERS --regional` in the repository to regenerate the comparison. The separately supplied extraction script uses the source files in the original workspace.
