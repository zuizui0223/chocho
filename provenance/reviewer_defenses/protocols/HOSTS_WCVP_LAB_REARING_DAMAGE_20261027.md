No new source taxonomy or locale name aliases. 80%-country status is a prespecified sensitivity. 2. For every source-accepted HOSTS interaction row with intact pair/location, preserve its literal `Lab Rearing` value (only `Y`, `?`, or empty are known) and `Damage` literal. Do not parse `Damage` as an independent life-stage label without verified encoding. Report exact 95% and 80% sensitivity and source host recorded indigenous/introduced classification, not a new country-definition optimization.

5. A catalogued `Lab Rearing=Y` means a **source row flagged for laboratory rearing by undocumented exact encoding**, NOT independently published wild larvae or an adult successfully emerged from a local host. A blank or '?' means unknown support, NOT a true absence or failed rearing.
