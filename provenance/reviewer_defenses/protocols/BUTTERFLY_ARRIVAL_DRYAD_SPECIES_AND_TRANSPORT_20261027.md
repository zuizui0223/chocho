## Two real public source products (not interchangeable)

**Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.dv41ns243**, Fenn-Moltu et al. (2023), latest corrected 31 Oct 2023. The source `transport_flows_updated.csv` contains intercepted **insect SPECIES RICHNESS** aggregated by origin ISO3 country × destination area × intercepted *commodity class* (14 broad categories) × decade. Its `establishment_flows.csv` contains established **insect species richness**, separately aggregated by insect *native biogeographic region* × destination × decade; not the identities of the same intercepted species. Historical periods, origin granularity and destinations differ. Do not perform `established_count / intercepted_count` as a biological probability; cannot recover person-level first arrival, or the same taxon's transition from interception to persistent establishment. Don't assume transport class is uniquely observed insect stage or nursery stock; first inspect `class` original values and README.

**Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.31zcrjdnw**, Mally et al. (2022), unintentional nonnative **LEPIDOPTERA** in 11 original establishment areas, species-level `S5_list_of_non-native_species.txt` (claimed 741 nonnative Lepidoptera). Additional original `S8_commodities.txt` and `S9_plant-commodities.txt` quantify border interceptions at LEPIDOPTERA-FAMILY × commodity level, which is a **different** observation grain and often different destination/period than S5. The original study 14 commodity classes and 10 nested plant-product HS classes (HS06 live plants/cut flowers; HS12 seeds/grains/medicinal; HS08 fruit/nuts; HS07 vegetables; HS10 cereals etc.). These are interceptions of butterflies **and moths**, many species nonfocal. Without a source species × intercepted-commodity link, **do not claim each original chocho butterfly was brought by any commodity**.
