At >=5 source butterfly records, **11/11 cases have at least one
plant record in a shared GADM1 administrative unit**. This is
compatible with a reporting-volume effect: more indexing
opportunities increase the chance of apparent co-detection.
It does NOT mean higher butterfly-recording effort causes co-occurrence
or that the plant actually supported the juvenile butterfly.

## Inferential limits

GBIF `hasCoordinate=true` and `hasGeospatialIssue=false` do not
verify native/wild plant occurrence, cultivation status, larval
encounter proximity, plant phenology, larval survival or successful
butterfly establishment. A negative plant facet records **no GBIF
observation under source filters**, not verified plant absence.
Each 26-cell outcome is **preselected to have indexed butterfly
records and source-added-host opportunity**, so it is not a
randomized set of positives/negatives for causal inference.

The original 229-taxonomic country analysis and its prospective
minimum of 50 informative species×region strata remain unchanged
(only 21 informative source strata). This 26-case plant co-detection
panel is data-source feasibility and conditional spatial
measurement rather than independent global butterfly evidence.
