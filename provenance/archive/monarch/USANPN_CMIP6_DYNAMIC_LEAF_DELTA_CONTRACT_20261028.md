# Climate-delta transfer test — leaf-status model conditional on warmed daily temperature

**2026-10-28, posthoc model-based simulation**. The current empirical result is that temperature+frost predicts directly observed milkweed live-leaf YES/NO at held-out NPN stations and held-out calendar years (PR #94). That does **not** identify leaf responses caused by temperature; this separate climate experiment must not be mistaken for climate-driven actual leaf phenology or monarch reproduction.

- Reuse the exact fixed six USA-NPN Asclepias `Leaves` taxa/phase ID 488 and 2018–2025 original response SHA receipts, 27,057 unique individual×date YES/NO responses, and **the exact 72 effort-selected NPN station IDs** from PR #93. No new species/locations or outcome-based site selection. Original 20,770 matched plant×date observations, 23 states, five taxa; no site coordinates, observers or raw source status records may leave process memory.
- Daymet original complete 8×365 daily `tmin`, `tmax`, `prcp` at all 72 stations, rechecked via aggregate SHA of original 72 JSON/CSV bodies from PR #93 and PR #94. Daymet source DOI https://doi.org/10.3334/ORNLDAAC/2361.
- Future CMIP6 original **18 WordClim 2.1 monthly Tmin/Tmax ZIPs** exactly SHA-matched PR #90 `monarch_cmip6_thermal_window_20261028.json`. Two GCMs MIROC6 and IPSL-CM6A-LR, SSP1-2.6/SSP5-8.5, 2041–2060/2081–2100, against original 1970–2000 WorldClim baseline. These are published downscaled climate fields; never invent a uniform warming increment.

## Frozen model and explicit transfer
1. Fit the previously validated **temperature-plus-frost only** L2 logistic (C=0.1), at all original 2018–2025 recorded plant visits using six original source taxa indicators, two annual DOY harmonic pairs, station latitude/longitude, calendar year, last-30d daily mean air temperature and last-30d number of Tmin <0°C days. StandardScaler fitted on historical visits only; original equal-station training weights. No precipitation model or new features are selected on future outcomes.
2. For each source station and calendar month compute original WorldClim future-minus-baseline 20-year climatological anomalies separately for `tmin` and `tmax`. Add anomalies to each **original Daymet 2018–2025 daily** Tmin/Tmax. Keep original Daymet precipitation, day-to-day variability, observation date, season, site, taxon and other covariates fixed. Recompute 30-day mean temperature and frost-day count from these daily hypothetical temperatures. Do NOT add a seasonal mean to frost **counts** or claim this is a full future daily weather sequence.
3. Use the fitted frozen model to predict probabilities of **source-visit-conditional Leaves=YES** for original and eight real CMIP6 anomaly panels. Average plant visits *within station*, then stations equally, for each species and calendar season. Report number of independent stations and original observed visits. The calendar/observed site cohort stays identical in all panels. Baseline model with original Daymet weather must exactly reproduce itself in the zero-anomaly control (1e-12 abs tolerance).
4. Under historical source weather define training-domain climate quantiles **1st–99th percentile** for each of 30-day mean temp and 30-day frost count. Report share of future source-visit covariates outside either band per species, season and scenario. **Primary numerical transfer gate** = physically valid CMIP6 at ≥95% original stations, no source SHA mismatch, **≤10%** future source visits outside either original weather band. If >10%, mark whole corresponding model×SSP×period result `UNSUPPORTED_EXTRAPOLATION`; any projections are conditional mathematical outputs only, never a supported forecast. This 10% test is a **posthoc transport guard**, not a preregistered statistical significance criterion.
5. Winter results (Nov–Feb) are estimable only if ≥10 unique source stations and ≥100 previously observed winter plant visits. Otherwise mark `INSUFFICIENT_WINTER_SITE_SAMPLE`; zero predicted winter visits does not mean winter absence.
6. Summer and other seasons are described using observed original visit times, not inferred whole-year future leafing start and end dates. Winter leaf accessibility to monarch larvae additionally depends on host biomass, leaf chemistry, irrigation and overwinter survival, none captured.

## Environmental and interpretation firewall
- Already established original PR #90 840 monarch-photo geographic climate study and PR #92 14-/9-site fixed observed leaf-month sensitivity are **different source sample frames**. Never multiply or merge their percentages with this 72-station future-perturbation output.
- Predictive gain in a historical observational model is **not a causal response to future climate**. Future climates may alter photoperiod-independent plant phenology, irrigation/cultivation, drought, site distributions, host abundance and the location of milkweed communities; this experiment keeps all of them fixed. Daymet 2018–2025 source meteorology differs from WorldClim historical 1970–2000 climate; the delta method is an assumption that reduces but cannot remove bias and site-regime differences.
- Monitored plants are convenience/volunteer records, not random host stems. No full-life-cycle monarch survival, number of generations, presence, population trend or warming benefits may be claimed. No statistical confidence interval may be inferred from just two GCMs.
