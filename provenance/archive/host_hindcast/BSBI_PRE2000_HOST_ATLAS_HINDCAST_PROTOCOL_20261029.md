# Source-locked pre-2000 UK larval host atlas for butterfly temporal prediction

Date: 2026-10-29. Independent exploratory PR, stacked on UKBMS LTMN PR #97. **Primary goal is to test whether independent, genuinely historical plant-host geography contains out-of-time predictive information beyond climate, calendar, geography and survey effort**; this is NOT a restatement of projected heat accumulation.

## Historic UK butterfly evidence and negative comparator

PR #97 source-fixed: 19 repeatedly visited georeferenced UKBMS transects, 27 preassigned chocho butterflies, 1981–95 original survey-based training, 2005–21 original monitored-visit testing, with focal-independent ≥3 other butterfly records on a visit (only conditional detection, not biological occupancy). 19 NASA POWER original daily 1981–2021 sources (coarse MERRA2) gave **worse** out-of-time log-loss than no weather: 0.410004→0.418668 (Δ −0.008664) and only 1/5 independent held-out transect folds positive. Hence a climate-only predictive model is NOT validated and cannot be projected to CMIP6 by itself. Freeze that negative source result; do not tune or reselect time windows based on future host model performance.

## Independent published botanical sources, fixed BEFORE opening content
- **BSBI Plant Atlas 2020** 10km×historic-period presence records, published April 2024, Zenodo DOI:10.5281/zenodo.10998003, exact published file `PA2020_10by10kmSquareData.zip`, declared MD5 `66a8bb4305df484839587b5fe8acf1d2`, expected archive ~10.4MB, original [record](https://zenodo.org/records/10998003). Content represents 3,497 taxonomic plant taxa at 10km hectads, from expert-collated *presence-only* records. The atlas includes pre-1930, 1930–49, 1950–69, 1970–86, 1987–99, 2000–09 and 2010–19 survey classes. Earlier recording is sparse/heterogeneous; lack of a record is NOT a surveyed botanical absence.
- **BSBI historical British/Irish plant native status** available separately at [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.11097371](https://zenodo.org/records/11097371), original archive `PA2020_NativeStatusData.zip`, declared MD5 `a5f60929e6118b77094110d6ca77bb48`. Native/alien **does not mean human-planted/maintained**; never interpret either as cause of butterfly persistence.
- **Independent European butterfly larval host taxon relations** Clarke (2024), Dryad DOI:10.5061/dryad.1vhhmgr12, source tables `larval_host_plant.csv`, `larval_plants.csv`, `larval_host_plant_status.csv`, [original record](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi%3A10.5061/dryad.1vhhmgr12). Source availability/licence must be confirmed; if unavailable, use published CC0 NHM HOSTS explicitly as a *different* source, and do not silently substitute hand-selected plant lists. The original chocho HOSTS/WCVP source may be used as a frozen author-only input if copyright/provenance permit; no raw author-only files committed.
- **Natural England LTMN vegetation** (published for approximately 50 permanent 2×2m plots at each site since circa 2009) [source](https://publications.naturalengland.org.uk/publication/6331137473904640); could validate **post-2009 observed plant presence** but it is not pre-2005 predictor information. Use only for external holdout-check of botanicals if site dates and taxa resolve.

## Pre-outcome identification and chronology rules
- Match 19 original UKBMS transect British National Grid E/N to the publisher's 10×10km hectad using a documented UK grid-reference converter. The centre of a butterfly transect is not a precise 10km botanical survey footprint; keep 10km scale and full locale mapping uncertainty. **Do not invent a plant presence at transect**, only presence reported somewhere in its 10km historical hectad.
- Source dates for primary host layer: **1970–1986 and earlier only**, no 1987–99, 2000–09, 2010–19. Independent secondary test: added 1987–1999 records are available before the 2005 test but AFTER 1995 butterfly training; label `PRETEST_1999_NOT_PRETRAIN_1995` and do not promote as a leak-free early-training covariate.
- No plant record means **unrecorded botanical status**; neither plant absence nor no food for larvae. Include **total number of taxa reported in the same 10km×era** as a botanical effort proxy. For each butterfly, compare historical documented-host count/share among equal-source taxonomic footprint with an equal-prevalence **nonhost plant placebo**, fixed before scoring butterfly outcomes.
- Host relation taxonomic precision must be binomial exact or source-validated synonym; genus-level broad host records unscorable. Host species relation documented at any geographic location ≠ accepted larval food in the UK, local plant quality, seasonal overlap or recruitment. Transparent taxonomic name match and equal-grid detection effort required; require ≥10 butterfly taxa with ≥1 exact host in atlas and ≥10/19 UKBMS transect grids with **some** original pre-2000 source plant records to declare pilot source-feasible.
- Primary future outcome 2005–2021 source UKBMS focal detection at verified actual survey visits, conditional on enough other butterflies being detected, not GBIF index count or extinction. Model comparison planned after source gate (no model scores opened yet): M0 historical survey time/season + region/species, M1 additionally historical NASA weather, M2 add **pre-test host availability**, and same-complexity nonhost placebo, all fitted 1981–95 and tested strictly 2005–21, plus held-out whole transects. The historical NASA M1 failure remains a permanent negative reference.
- **Original hypothesis about human-maintained/alien hosts is NOT testable from native-status/10km botanical reports alone**. A dated planted/cultivated original host layer (species identity and management intensity) must be sourced and analysed independently; UK LTMN `HABITAT1/2` cannot establish actual human-maintained host plant species or timing.
- Study source missingness, taxonomic coverage, provider restrictions and lack of host-history contrasts across 19 sites must be reported regardless of whether result is positive.
