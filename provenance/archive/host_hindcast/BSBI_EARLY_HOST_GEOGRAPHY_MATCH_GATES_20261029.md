# Fixed source-era host exposure & botanical-effort gate before opening original BSBI × UKBMS site overlap

2026-10-29, exploratory source-validity addendum. Original BSBI [ZIP source audit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/actions/runs/40803639278) verified all **3,497 taxon CSVs**, **2,608,461** taxon×hectad rows, six period columns and exact literal **0/1** indicators. These are *presence recorded during period* indicators, not evidence a 0 taxon was not present. Original author-only HOSTS [source audit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/actions/runs/40803438021) verified exactly **27/27** butterfly taxa with 377 distinct accepted WCVP host binomials, author-source CSV SHA256 `0a084fb5273e4780b03e015205c87e4545c69f0f8e517feb1a2a3879889508e9`. No local historic butterfly outcomes were used in these two botanical identities.

This next source-only join is fixed *before* evaluating any host–butterfly endpoint:

- Use only original 1981–95 and 2005–21 repeated UKBMS transects selected via actual survey effort and original stable British National Grid coordinates; source overlap expected 15–19. Convert BNG East/North directly to 10 km British National Grid hectad names; verify known grid test points (London TQ38, Edinburgh NT27).
- Join WCVP source accepted **exact binomial** to BSBI source `taxonName` exactly (no fuzzy genus, taxon authority or outcome-directed synonym choices). A source match is eligible only if its BSBI `rank` is species and a unique named BSBI taxon file ID resolves. Report unmatched/synonym matches as missing identity, *not absence of larvae/hosts*.
- Strictly historical predictor `pre1970`: BSBI period flags `to 1929` and `1930 - 1969` only. Additional source-only descriptive `pre1987` includes `1970 - 1986` but this MUST NOT be inserted as a fixed covariate into butterfly training visits earlier than 1987 (future info leak).
- Count **documented host plant species** (positive cells) per site×focal butterfly and **all documented wild plant species** in the same BSBI 10 km hectad (crude botanical sampling-effort proxy). No plant identity is treated as absent merely because 0 observed; all positive source recorded taxa are observational and not directly measured butterfly larval host use.
- Prior to any M0 vs M1 model score, require >=10 historically surveyed and mapped UK transects, >=5 distinct valid 10km hectads, >=15 exact host-binomial-to-BSBI match taxa, and >=8 focal butterfly species with *between-source-transect* variation in the historical host-positive count. Failure means no host model; success only means the historical model is feasible and a separately frozen M0/M1 contest may be attempted.
- Do NOT infer human-maintained or human-planted hosts from BSBI wild-plant records. The source explicitly excludes most gardens and agricultural crops. For human-mediated hypothesis, independently dated horticultural / agricultural host occurrence records are still needed.
- No site IDs or coordinates, exact source original plant×hectad rows, recorders, original HOSTS interactions or per-site plant identities are committed; only per-butterfly aggregate coverage, source SHA and gates.

Original completed NASA weather baseline on 1981–95→2005–21 [Action #40802452296](https://github.com/zuizui0223/chocho/actions/runs/40802452296) did NOT pass historical weather predictive skill (site-species equal heldout logloss **0.410004 without weather vs 0.418668 with original NASA POWER weather**, 1/5 positive spatial/temporal site folds). This weakens the model's climate component, and means that even a later M1 improvement cannot be described as validated *climate-mediated* host rescue without an improved meteorological baseline. Do not retune the climate model on the 2005–21 outcomes.
